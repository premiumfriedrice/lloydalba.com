---
title: On building in public
date: Mar 2026
---

Shipping in public is a forcing function. When the work is visible, the
standard you hold yourself to quietly rises to meet the audience — even if
the audience is three people and a bot.

The downside is the temptation to polish instead of ship. The cure is the
same as everywhere else: small releases, honest notes, and letting the
rough edges show what you learned.
